How to make a logo for free in 5 steps

  1. Type your business name. Add a short tagline if it helps people understand what you do, like “Plumbing & Drain Repair” or “Atlanta, GA”.
  2. Pick a font. Choose one that fits your brand. Clean sans-serifs like Montserrat or Inter feel modern. Serifs like Playfair Display feel classic and premium. Scripts like Pacifico feel friendly and handmade.
  3. Choose an icon and shape. Search 300+ icons, then put the icon in a circle, shield, hexagon or badge, or leave it on its own.
  4. Set your colors. Start with a palette, then adjust each color. Two or three colors are usually enough.
  5. Download. Get a transparent PNG for your website, an SVG for printing and resizing, a JPG for places that don't accept transparency, and a favicon pack for your browser tab icon.

Not sure where to start? Press Inspire me to generate random ideas from your name, or open a starter template and tweak it.

Logo design tips that actually matter

Keep it simple

The best small-business logos are recognizable at a glance: one icon, one or two fonts and a few colors. Your logo will show up as a tiny social profile picture, a Google Business Profile photo and a browser tab icon. Fine details and long taglines disappear at those sizes.

Make more than one version

Most brands need a horizontal logo (icon left of the name) for website headers and email signatures, a stacked logo (icon on top) for square spaces, and an icon-only mark for favicons and profile pictures. Switch layouts here and download each one. Your colors and fonts carry over.

Check it at small sizes

Set the canvas to Favicon or Social profile picture and look at the preview from a distance. If you can't read the name, shorten the tagline, use a bolder weight or rely on the icon.

Pair fonts carefully

A reliable formula is a bold, characterful font for the name and a simple sans-serif in uppercase with extra letter spacing for the tagline. Avoid pairing two script or two display fonts. They compete for attention.

Use color with contrast

Pick colors that stand out from each other: dark text on a light background, or light text on a dark one. Also check that your logo works in one color, because you'll eventually need it on a T-shirt, a stamp or a fax-style invoice.

Logo file formats explained

FormatTransparent?Best for
PNGYesWebsites, social media, Canva, presentations and documents. Use 2× resolution for sharp results on high-resolution screens.
SVGYesA vector file that stays sharp at any size. Use it for print shops, signs, vehicle wraps, embroidery and web developers.
JPGNoPlaces that only accept JPG, email attachments and photo uploads. JPG always has a solid background.
ICO / favicon PNGsYesThe small icon in browser tabs, bookmarks and phone home screens.

If a printer or sign shop asks for a “vector file”, send the SVG. If they need an AI or EPS file, open the SVG in Illustrator, Inkscape or Affinity Designer and save it in that format.

Transparent background tips

  • Turn on “Transparent background” before you download a PNG or SVG. The checkerboard in the preview shows which areas will be see-through.
  • Think about where the logo will sit. Dark text vanishes on a dark website header. Download a light version for dark backgrounds by changing the colors and downloading again.
  • Don't use JPG for transparency. JPG can't store it, so this tool fills JPG downloads with your background color, or white if transparency is on.
  • Use a shape for busy backgrounds. Putting your icon in a filled circle or badge keeps it readable on photos and patterned backgrounds.
  • Apple touch icons need a background. iPhones show transparent areas as black, so the 180 px icon in the favicon pack always gets your background color.

FAQ

Free logo maker questions

Is this logo maker really free?

Yes. Designing and downloading are free, with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on downloads. You can download PNG, SVG and JPG files and a favicon pack, and use them for your business, including commercially.

Can I make a logo with a transparent background?

Yes. Turn on Transparent background in the Canvas tab. The preview shows a checkerboard where the background will be see-through. Then download a PNG or SVG. JPG files can't be transparent, so JPG downloads always get a solid background.

Can I use the logo commercially, and can I trademark it?

You can use the files you create for your business. The icons come from the open-source Lucide set (ISC license) and the fonts are Google Fonts under open licenses, so both are free for commercial use. Anyone can use the same icons and fonts, though, so a logo built from them may not be distinctive enough to register as a trademark. If you need a one-of-a-kind, trademark-ready mark, have a designer create a custom symbol.

Is my design saved?

Your design is saved automatically in this browser on this device, so you can close the page and pick up where you left off. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Clearing your browser data or switching devices starts a fresh design, so download your files when you're happy with them.

Can I edit the SVG in Canva, Figma, Illustrator or Inkscape?

Yes. The SVG keeps your icon and shapes as sharp vectors. The text uses the Google Font you picked, embedded in the file. Some editors swap in a different font when they open an SVG. If that happens, install the same free Google Font (its name is shown in the Text tab) and the logo will match.

What is included in the favicon pack?

A ZIP with favicon.ico (16, 32 and 48 px), favicon-16x16.png, favicon-32x32.png, favicon-48x48.png, a 180 px apple-touch-icon.png, 192 and 512 px Android icons, a site.webmanifest file and a README with the HTML to paste into your site. The favicon uses your icon mark (or your first letter if you have no icon), because full wordmarks are unreadable at 16 pixels.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The logo maker runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers. On a phone, the preview sits above the controls and downloads go to your Downloads folder or Files app.
