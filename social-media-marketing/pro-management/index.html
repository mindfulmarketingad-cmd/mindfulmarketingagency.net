Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Starter and Pro?

Starter plans deliver daily designed posts on a set number of platforms. Pro plans add a manager who handles strategy, publishing, and (on 30-day plans) engagement and reporting.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Each plan is a one-time payment. Most clients renew month to month once they see the results.

Do you run paid ads in these plans?

No, these are organic management plans. Paid campaigns are a separate service: Facebook & Instagram ads.

Which platforms can you manage?

Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, Pinterest, and YouTube Community. You pick them on the intake form.

How do you get access to my accounts?

You add us as a manager or partner on your business accounts, so you keep ownership and can remove access at any time.
