Frequently asked questions

Which platforms do you post to?

Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Pinterest, TikTok, or Google Business Profile — you choose on the intake form, up to the number of platforms in your plan.

Do I need to give you my passwords?

Usually not. In most cases you add us as a page manager or connect a scheduling tool, so you stay in control of your accounts.

Do you reply to comments and messages?

Starter plans focus on content and scheduling. For engagement and community management, choose a Pro plan.

Is this a subscription?

No. Each plan is a one-time purchase. Buy another plan when you want to keep going.

Can I approve posts before they go live?

Yes. Tell us on the intake form and we will share the content for approval before scheduling.
