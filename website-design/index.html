Frequently asked questions

What platform do you build on?

Most sites are built on WordPress, which you can easily update yourself. Online stores can be built with WooCommerce. Tell us if you have a preference.

Is hosting included?

Hosting and your domain are not included, and we recommend they be in your name. We will help you pick an affordable host and set everything up.

Do I need to write the content?

You know your business best, so we ask for the basics — services, about info, and photos. We polish the copy and structure it for SEO.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Choose the package that matches the number of pages you need and mention your current site on the intake form.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the size and how quickly content comes in. We confirm a timeline with you before we start.
