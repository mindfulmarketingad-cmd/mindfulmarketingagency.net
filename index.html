About

Hi, I'm David — founder of Mindful Marketing Agency.

I grew up in Stone Mountain, GA and have called the Atlanta area home my whole life. I went on to earn a degree in Information Systems and Marketing from Georgia Southern University — a combination that gave me both the analytical backbone and the creative toolkit that shapes how I approach every client.

I started this studio on a simple belief: great marketing doesn't shout, it resonates. For nearly a decade I've helped founders and small teams turn scattered ideas into clear, consistent brands that earn attention honestly. At the core of everything I do is a genuine love for adding value to people's lives and a drive to motivate others to reach what they're capable of.

"Mindful" isn't a buzzword here — it's how I work. That means strategy before tactics, listening before launching, and measuring what matters instead of chasing every trend. The result is marketing that feels like you and performs like it should. Good strategy travels, so while I'm proud to be Atlanta-based, I partner with clients all over the country.

  • Strategy-first, data-informed, human-centered
  • One dedicated point of contact — me
  • Transparent reporting on the metrics that move revenue
