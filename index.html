No. SEO buildouts and citation packages are one-time purchases, and ongoing services are month-to-month.

We're Atlanta-based and focus on metro Atlanta — Buckhead, Midtown, Decatur, Sandy Springs, Marietta, Stone Mountain, Alpharetta and more — but we work with small businesses anywhere in Georgia and beyond.

Citation packages are delivered in 4–5 days, and SEO buildouts go live as soon as your pages are approved. Rankings themselves build over weeks to months, which is why we start with the foundations Google looks for first.
