About This Directory

Georgia's directory of marketing agencies

Georgia Marketing Agencies is a free, searchable directory of marketing agencies based in or serving Georgia — built from real Google Business listings, including each agency's rating, review count, hours, address, phone number, and website. Instead of guessing which agency to call first, you can browse every SEO, PPC, social media, web design, branding, content, and email marketing agency in the state in one place.

Use the search bar above, or the city and service pages, to filter and sort agencies until you land on the exact fit — narrow results down to a specific city like Atlanta or Savannah, a specific service like SEO or PPC, or a specific industry niche like ecommerce or restaurant marketing. Every listing links out to a full profile with contact info, so you can reach out directly — no lead forms or gatekeeping in between.

Beyond the directory, we also offer our own marketing services — done-for-you SEO, digital marketing, and social media marketing, if you'd rather work with us directly instead of browsing listings.
