Our SEO buildout is a one-time $49.99 (Foundation), $99.99 (Foundation + Content), or $199.99 (Full Buildout). Local citation packages are $35 for 50, $55 for 100, or $160 for 300 citations. No monthly contract is required.

What is the difference between the SEO buildout and citations?

The SEO buildout sets up Google Search Console and Analytics and creates keyword-optimized pages on your site. Citations are listings of your business name, address, and phone on other trusted directories, which help you rank in Google Maps. Most local businesses benefit from both.

Do you offer SEO content marketing?

Yes. Tier 2 and Tier 3 SEO buildouts include keyword-rich pages, and our content marketing service adds ongoing blog posts and service-page copy targeted to Atlanta searches.
