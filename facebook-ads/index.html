Frequently asked questions

Is the ad budget included?

No. Our fee covers setup and management. You pay Meta directly for ad spend from your own ad account.

How much should I spend on Facebook ads?

Many local businesses start with $10–$30 a day. We will recommend a budget based on your goals and audience size.

Do you create the images and videos?

We write the ad copy and create ad creative from your photos, logo, and brand. If you have product photos or videos, send them — real footage usually performs best.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. We work through your Meta Business Manager as a partner, and you can remove access at any time.
