Frequently asked questions

Is ad spend included in the price?

No. Our fee covers setup and management. Your ad budget is paid directly to Google from your own account, so you control it.

How much should I spend on Google Ads in Atlanta?

It depends on your industry and competition. Many local service businesses start around $15–$50 a day. We will give you a recommendation based on keyword costs during research.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

You do. We work inside your account, and everything we build stays with you.

Do you guarantee results?

We cannot guarantee a number of leads, because results depend on your offer, budget, and market. We do guarantee a clean build, proper tracking, and clear reporting.

Can I continue management after the package?

Yes. Ask us about ongoing month-to-month management once your package wraps up.
