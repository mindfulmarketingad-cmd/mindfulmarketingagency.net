About the Directory

A directory built for Georgia businesses

Finding the right marketing agency shouldn't mean sifting through pages of generic search results. This directory organizes Georgia's marketing agencies by city and by service, so you can compare real options fast.

About Mindful Marketing Agency

Mindful Marketing Agency is an Atlanta, GA digital marketing agency for small businesses. We sell flat-priced, done-for-you packages — SEO, local citations, Google Business Profile optimization, backlinks, social media management, Google Ads, Facebook & Instagram ads, and website design — with no long-term contracts and one point of contact for every project. See all services and pricing.

What this directory is

Georgia Marketing Agencies is a free, searchable directory of marketing agencies operating across the state — from Atlanta to Savannah, Augusta to Athens. Each listing includes the agency's service specialty, location, rating, and contact details, pulled together in one place.

How listings are organized

Agencies are organized two ways: by city, so you can find agencies based in or serving your area, and by service, so you can find agencies that specialize in exactly what you need — SEO, paid media, social media, web design, branding, content, or email marketing.

Listing your agency

If you run a marketing agency in Georgia and don't see your business listed, you can add it for free. Submissions are reviewed before going live.
