Frequently asked questions

Will backlinks get me to #1 on Google?

No honest agency can promise a specific ranking. Backlinks are one of the strongest ranking factors, and these packages add real authority, but results depend on your competition, your site, and your content. We recommend pairing links with on-page SEO.

Are these links safe?

We build links on real, indexed sites at a natural pace and vary the anchor text. We avoid the things that get sites in trouble, like private blog networks sold as "guaranteed DA 90" links.

How long does delivery take?

About 18 days for Foundation, 25 days for Pillar, and 34 days for Tower. Links are built gradually on purpose.

Can I choose which pages get links?

Yes. You choose the URLs and keywords on the intake form after checkout. Foundation covers up to 3 keywords, Pillar up to 5, and Tower up to 10.

Do you work with businesses outside Atlanta?

Yes. We are based in Atlanta and focus on Georgia, but backlink packages work for any English-language website.
