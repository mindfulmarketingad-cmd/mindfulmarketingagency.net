Frequently asked questions

Do you need to own my profile?

No. You add us as a manager, we do the work, and you can remove us afterwards. You always stay the owner.

What if I don’t have a profile yet or it isn’t verified?

Let us know on the form. We can help set it up, but Google requires the owner to complete verification.

Will this get me into the top 3 on Google Maps?

Optimization makes your profile as strong as possible, but map rankings also depend on your distance from the searcher, reviews, and competition. We never promise a specific position.

Do you write fake reviews?

No — never. Fake reviews violate Google’s rules and can get your profile suspended. We can help you set up a simple process to ask real customers for reviews.
