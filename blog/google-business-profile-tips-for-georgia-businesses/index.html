Google Business Profile Tips for Georgia Businesses

For many Georgia businesses, the Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) gets seen more often than the website. It's what appears in Google Maps and the map results in search, with your reviews, hours, photos, and a call button front and center. It's also free.

Here are the tips that make the biggest difference, in roughly the order we'd tackle them.

1. Claim and Verify Your Profile

Search for your business on Google Maps. If a profile exists, claim it; if not, create one at business.google.com. Google may ask you to verify by video, phone, text, email, or postcard depending on your business. Video verification usually means recording a short clip showing your signage, your location, and proof you manage the business, so have those ready.

2. Pick the Right Primary Category

Your primary category is one of the most important ranking signals you control. Choose the most specific one that accurately describes your core business ("Family law attorney" rather than "Lawyer"; "Mexican restaurant" rather than "Restaurant"). Add a few secondary categories only if they genuinely apply.

3. Use Your Real Business Name

It's tempting to add keywords: "Smith Plumbing | Best Plumber Atlanta GA". Don't. Google's guidelines require your real-world business name, and keyword-stuffed names are a common reason profiles get suspended. Put keywords in your services and description instead.

4. Fill Out Every Field

  • Hours, including special hours for holidays
  • Services or products, each with a short description
  • Business description that says what you do and the areas you serve, in plain language
  • Attributes that apply (for example, women-owned, veteran-owned, wheelchair accessible)
  • Website link and appointment link, pointing to the most relevant pages

If you serve customers at their location rather than yours, hide your address and set a service area covering the Georgia cities or counties you actually serve.

5. Add Real Photos, Regularly

Profiles with real, recent photos look more trustworthy than those with stock images or none at all. Add your exterior (so people can find you), interior, team, and examples of your work. A few new photos a month keeps the profile looking active.

6. Build a Review Habit

Reviews influence both rankings and whether people choose you. Create a short review link from your profile dashboard and send it by text or email after every completed job. Reply to every review: thank happy customers by name, and respond to negative reviews calmly with an offer to make it right offline.

Never pay for reviews, offer discounts in exchange for them, or post reviews for yourself. Google actively removes fake reviews and can restrict profiles that solicit them.

7. Post Updates

Google Business Profile posts let you share offers, events, and updates directly on your listing. They're a simple way to show the business is active. Aim for a post every week or two: a recent project, a seasonal tip, or an upcoming event in your town.

8. Track What It's Doing

The Performance section shows how many people found you, which searches surfaced your profile, and how many called, requested directions, or visited your website. To see profile visitors in your website analytics, add UTM parameters to your website link (for example ?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp).

Common Mistakes Georgia Businesses Make

  • Using a virtual office or PO box as the address, which violates Google's guidelines
  • Creating duplicate profiles for the same location
  • Letting hours go stale over holidays and seasonal changes
  • Inconsistent name, address, or phone compared with the website and other listings (see local citations explained)
  • Setting it up once and never touching it again

Frequently asked questions

Is Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Creating, verifying, and managing a Google Business Profile is free.

How long does Google Business Profile verification take?

It varies by method and business. Video, phone, and email verification can be quick; postcard verification can take a couple of weeks. Some profiles also go through additional review.

Can I have a Google Business Profile without a storefront?

Yes. Service-area businesses can hide their address and list the areas they serve instead.
