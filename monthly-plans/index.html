Frequently asked questions

Is there a contract?

No. Both plans are billed monthly and you can cancel anytime. Cancel before your next billing date and you won’t be charged again.

When does work start?

Right after checkout you’ll get a short onboarding form. We start within two business days of receiving it, and the first month’s work is delivered within about three weeks.

Do I have to give you my passwords?

No. For Google Business Profile we ask you to add us as a manager, and for social media we use your platform’s own team or partner access where it exists. You stay the owner of every account.

Can I switch or combine plans?

Yes. Many local businesses run both. Cancel one and start the other at any time, or email us and we’ll set it up for you.

Do you guarantee rankings or followers?

No — and you should be wary of anyone who does. We guarantee the work listed for each plan, delivered every month, plus a report showing what was done.
