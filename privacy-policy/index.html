Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mindful Marketing Agency ("we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit mindfulmarketingagency.net (the "Site") or buy our services.

Information we collect

  • Information you submit. When you fill out a request, proposal, contact, or agency-listing form, we collect what you enter, such as your name, email, phone number, website, business details, and message. Form submissions are stored securely with our database provider, Supabase.
  • Payment information. Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number. Stripe handles your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive limited details such as your name, email, amount, and purchase.
  • Usage data and cookies. Like most websites, our hosting provider and third-party services may automatically collect information such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referring site. Cookies and similar technologies may be used for this.
  • Free tools. Our free logo maker runs in your browser. Logos you design are not uploaded to our servers.

How we use information

  • To reply to your request and deliver the services you buy.
  • To process payments, send receipts, and keep business records.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Site.
  • To show advertising, as described below.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business (such as Supabase, Stripe, our hosting provider Vercel, and specialists who help fulfill your project under confidentiality), or when required by law.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads on this Site. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Your choices

You can set your browser to refuse or delete cookies. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you've given us by emailing David@mindfulmarketingagency.net. We'll respond within two business days.

Data retention and security

We keep information as long as needed to provide services and meet legal and accounting obligations, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

The Site is meant for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions? Email David@mindfulmarketingagency.net or use our contact page.
