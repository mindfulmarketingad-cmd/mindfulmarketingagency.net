Website Maintenance Services in Atlanta Georgia

Most website problems are preventable. A missed update becomes a security hole, a full database slows every page load, an expired SSL certificate scares away visitors. Website maintenance catches these issues on a schedule — before they turn into a call at 11pm because your site is down.

Malware & vulnerability scanning

SSL certificate monitoring

Broken link & 404 checks

Database cleanup & optimization

Plugin compatibility testing

Staging environment testing
